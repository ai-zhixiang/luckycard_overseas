About boon-enc v1 - the webmaster's own algorithm (click to read)
boon-enc v1 is an original cipher designed by the webmaster - no standard algorithm is used. It wraps the text in many nested rounds of simple reversible transforms, burying the plaintext deeper each time.
Q4 = 10 rounds · Q5 = 8 rounds · Q8 = 34 rounds (~209 KB file).

Every transform is reversible, but the round recipe is secret. Cracking it means peeling every layer blind - good luck.
